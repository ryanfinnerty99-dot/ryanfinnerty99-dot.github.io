Ultron privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

  • Ultron is used only by its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.
  • Ultron asks Google only for read-only access to YouTube Analytics for the owner's own channel. It never posts, uploads, edits or deletes anything on the channel.
  • Data received from Google is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sold, shared, or sent to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.
  • Ultron's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
